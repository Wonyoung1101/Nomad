Automatic trip planning

Plan your trip around your budget.

Nomad helps you build a smarter itinerary based on your destination, travel dates, and spending style. It creates a complete travel plan with recommended areas, activities, food, transportation, and hotel options shaped around how much you want to spend.

Enter trip details Build itinerary Review results Download PDF

How it works

  1. Enter your destination, travel dates, arrival time, departure time, and total budget.
  2. Click Build My Trip.
  3. Review the generated stay, itinerary, and budget summary.
  4. Download the itinerary as a PDF and save it to your phone or laptop.
